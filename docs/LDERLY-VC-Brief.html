Problem + Market

Families need continuous assurance, not another service directory.

The pain

  • Working professionals and NRI families cannot reliably monitor parents throughout the day.
  • Care services are fragmented across attendants, labs, pharmacies, doctors, emergency contacts, and family members.
  • Most elder care apps behave like static booking platforms, with weak live tracking and poor operational accountability.
  • Emergency response is usually manual, delayed, and difficult for families to verify remotely.

Why now

  • India’s ageing population is growing while adult children are increasingly mobile and urban/NRI.
  • Families already trust app-led logistics for rides, food, payments, and delivery. Elder care is ready for the same operational layer.
  • Realtime location, push, payments, AI summaries, and low-cost cloud infrastructure make a care OS viable.
  • Trust, verification, and operational proof can become the moat.
Product thesis: the winning elder care platform will not be a marketplace. It will be a realtime trust and operations layer for families.

Seniors

Product System

Three coordinated apps on one backend.

CustomerBooks care for parent/self, pays, tracks caregiver, receives updates.
DispatchOps monitors booking, risk, SLA, assignment, escalation.
CaregiverAccepts assignment, shares location, starts via OTP, uploads care proof.
Live CareFamily sees ETA, visit progress, notes, medicines, vitals, reports.
CompletionCustomer verifies, rating/rebook, payment release workflow.

Customer app

  • Lead registration and customer login
  • Care recipient flow: mother, father, self/others
  • Guided booking funnel by care need, service, duration, location
  • Payment terms modal and Razorpay checkout path
  • Realtime care tracking with live route ETA foundation
  • Trust UI: caregiver verification, visit proof, family updates

Caregiver app

  • Partner login and assignment view
  • Accept/reject workflow hidden from customer until assigned
  • Mark en route, arrived, start visit, complete visit
  • OTP start verification
  • Vitals, medication, proof upload, incident and SOS workflows
  • Android native foundation for background GPS

Admin / Ops command center

Live bookings, caretaker assignment, SLA risk, emergency queue, escalation, audit logs, operational analytics, lead management, Google sync health, backup/recovery, support workflows, finance actions, and readiness checks.
